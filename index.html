Louisville Home Inspectors Who Know the Local Housing Stock

A Louisville home inspection is different from one in a newer suburb. Our home inspectors regularly work in century-old homes in Old Louisville, the Highlands, and Germantown, as well as mid-century ranches in Shively and Okolona and newer builds in Prospect, Middletown, and Oldham County. Each era has its own common problems, like original clay-tile sewer laterals, Federal Pacific panels, polybutylene supply lines, and damp crawl spaces sitting on Ohio Valley clay.

Every Louisville home inspector on our team is licensed by the Kentucky Board of Home Inspectors, carries the required insurance, and follows a recognized standards of practice. You'll get a clear, photo-rich report the same day, plus time to walk through the findings and ask questions before your inspection contingency runs out.

Home Inspection FAQs — Louisville

Common questions about home inspections in the Louisville area

We charge fixed prices by finished square footage: $299 up to 1,200 sq ft, $349 up to 2,000 sq ft, $429 up to 3,000 sq ft, $499 up to 4,000 sq ft, and $579 up to 5,000 sq ft. Most home inspections take 2 to 4 hours depending on the size and condition of the property. Older Louisville homes with basements, additions, or knob-and-tube-era wiring can take longer. We encourage buyers to attend so we can walk through the findings with you in person.
Jefferson, Oldham, and Bullitt Counties all sit in EPA Radon Zone 1, the highest-potential category. Kentucky does not require a radon test as part of a home sale, so it is the buyer's decision — but we recommend it on every purchase. Added to an inspection, our radon test is a fixed $150.
Yes. Kentucky licenses home inspectors under KRS 198B.700 to 198B.738. A licensed inspector must complete 80 hours of board-approved pre-licensing education, pass the National Home Inspector Examination, carry at least $250,000 in general liability insurance, and complete continuing education each renewal cycle. Every inspection arranged through Louisville Home Inspection Services is performed by an inspector who meets those Kentucky requirements, and you can ask for license and insurance verification before your appointment.
We deliver your full inspection report the same day the inspection is completed — typically within a few hours of leaving the property.
